How I run Google Ads

Every click has to earn its place

Most of my accounts ran on $15–20 a day. Tight budgets taught me discipline: no wasted clicks, no guesswork, and everything measured against qualified leads.

01

Tracking first

Conversion tracking goes live before any campaign does. Microsoft Clarity heatmaps and recordings show how real users behave.

02

CPC discipline

Average CPC stays under $5, so expensive clicks don't starve the keywords that actually convert.

03

Negatives from day one

Informational searches like "how," "why," "free" and "DIY" are blocked at launch and refined from search term reports.

04

A/B testing ad copy

Multiple ad variations run side by side with split budgets. Winners get consolidated and scaled.

05

Daily optimization

Bids, budgets, match types and keywords are reviewed every day to keep spend focused on qualified leads.

06

Strategy fits the business

Shopping and product campaigns for e-commerce. High-intent Search with matched landing pages for service businesses.
